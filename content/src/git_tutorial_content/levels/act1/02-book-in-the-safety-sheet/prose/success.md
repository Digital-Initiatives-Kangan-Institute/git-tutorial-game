There is a phrase on your screen you will be glad to see for the rest
of your career: nothing to commit, working tree clean.

It means every change you have made is recorded, and git has nothing
outstanding to tell you about. Run `git log --oneline`: your commit is
at the top, in the same format as Marta's and Theo's, carrying the
same weight.

The two steps are the thing to take away. Staging is where you choose,
committing is where you record. Next job, you check what is on the
bench before you book it in.
