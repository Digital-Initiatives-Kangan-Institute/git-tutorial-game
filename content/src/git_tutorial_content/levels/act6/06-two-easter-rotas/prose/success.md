One rota, four days, and both drafts in the history.

An add/add conflict looks alarming because the whole file seems to be
in dispute. It is really the plainest kind: two people did the same job
at once. `git log --merge` told you who, and the answer was to keep
both.

Not every conflict has a winner. Choosing a side whole is right when
one side is better; combining is right when both are half the job.
Next job: a conflict that arrives at the worst possible moment.
