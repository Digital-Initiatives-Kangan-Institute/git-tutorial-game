The sheet is your scan exactly, and Theo's log line came in beside it.

`--ours` and `--theirs` are the same tool pointed in opposite
directions. Which way to point it is never git's decision: it is a
question about whose information is better, and here the answer was the
fresher count.

Notice what you did not do: `git push --force` would also have left
your sheet at Head Office, and taken Theo's commit and his log line
with it. Next job: a sheet that was not changed at Head Office, but
removed.
