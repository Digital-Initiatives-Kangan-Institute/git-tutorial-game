There is the merge commit nobody types. Two lines that split
yesterday, your commit on one, Ines's on the other, and a commit
joining them at the top.

When pull does something you did not expect, ask which half did it:
did fetch bring something you were not ready for, or did the merge
combine things in a way you would not have chosen?

Pull before you start, not after you finish. This merge was painless
because you and Ines touched different files. Next job: you push
without pulling, and Head Office says no.
