Handed in. Your report is at Head Office on a branch of its own, your
rough notes are still on your disk, and nothing was attached to
anything.

The work is finished when it is somewhere other than your machine, in
a place the people who need it have agreed to look.

You can now read a repository, book work into it, undo mistakes at
every stage, and share the result with other people. Underneath all of
it: a commit is a snapshot, a branch is a label pointing at one, and
everything that moves a label is written down. When in doubt, look
first: `git status`, `git log --oneline --graph --all`, `git reflog`.
